The first test version.

- **First-time setup** walks you through adding your Steam API key and finding your profile.
- **Overview** counts your games, game badges, foil badges and inventory items, with a breakdown by item type.
- **Test the Steam connection** runs a short, safe check of every Steam source the app will use and saves a report.
- **Themes:** greyscale, dark and light, in Settings.
- **Updates itself** when new versions are published.

Completion percentages, full item lists and prices come in the next versions.
